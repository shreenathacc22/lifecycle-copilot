Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · Applies to memorymanager.stream

Short version: this site is a single information page. It has no sign-up, no forms, no analytics and no advertising. We don't ask for any personal information. Like every website, the services that deliver it see your IP address and basic request details. This page explains which services those are.

Who runs this site

memorymanager.stream is run by Shreenath Shetty as a personal project, a product page for Lifecycle Copilot, an IT asset lifecycle tool in pilot. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the operator.

Contact for anything about privacy: shreenathacc22@gmail.com

What we collect

We don't collect anything you type or submit, because the site has nothing to type into. We don't use cookies of our own, tracking pixels, analytics tools or browser storage.

When you load a page, the server records a standard access log entry. It contains the time, the page requested, the response code, your browser's user-agent string, and the address the request came from (usually a Cloudflare address rather than yours, because Cloudflare sits in front of the server). We use these logs only to keep the site running and secure. They're deleted automatically after about 10 days by routine log rotation. We don't sell them, share them or combine them with anything else.

Lifecycle Copilot is not offered for download or use on this site. The sample rows shown on the home page are made up, not real assets. No customer or company inventory data is published or processed here.

Children

This site is for IT professionals and isn't aimed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Your choices and rights

You can block cookies and fonts in your browser, and the page will still be readable. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, or to have it deleted. Because we hold almost nothing (short-lived server logs at most), the honest answer is usually "nothing tied to you". You're still welcome to ask at the address above, and we'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If the site starts collecting anything new, such as a sign-up form or analytics, this policy will be updated before that change goes live, and the effective date above will change.

Privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026 · Applies to memorymanager.stream

Short version: this site has one form, the Lifecycle Copilot waitlist. If you join, we store your email, the role you picked (if any), when you joined, and the sentence you agreed to. We use it only to tell you about Lifecycle Copilot. No analytics, no advertising, no selling or sharing. Like every website, the services that deliver it see your IP address and basic request details. This page explains which.

Who runs this site

memorymanager.stream is run by Shreenath Shetty as a personal project, a product page for Lifecycle Copilot, an IT asset lifecycle tool in pilot. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the operator.

Contact for anything about privacy: shreenathacc22@gmail.com

What we collect

If you join the waitlist: your email address, the role you chose from the list (optional), the date and time you joined, and the exact consent sentence shown next to the button. That's all. We don't store your IP address or browser details with it. We don't use cookies of our own, tracking pixels, analytics tools or browser storage.

When you load a page, the server records a standard access log entry. It contains the time, the page requested, the response code, your browser's user-agent string, and the address the request came from (usually a Cloudflare address rather than yours, because Cloudflare sits in front of the server). We use these logs only to keep the site running and secure. They're deleted automatically after about 10 days by routine log rotation. We don't sell them, share them or combine them with anything else.

The waitlist

We keep the waitlist in a database on our own server (hosted by Amazon Web Services in the US), not with a mailing-list company. We use it only to email you about Lifecycle Copilot: when there's something to try, and the occasional update. We don't sell it, share it or add you to anything else. Before we send any bulk email, we'll ask you to confirm your address, and every email will have an unsubscribe link.

We keep your entry until you ask us to remove it or we close the waitlist. A daily backup is kept for 7 days, so a deleted email is gone from backups within a week. To be removed, email the address above. We'll delete it and confirm within 30 days.

Lifecycle Copilot is not offered for download or use on this site. The sample rows shown on the home page are made up, not real assets. No customer or company inventory data is published or processed here.

Children

This site is for IT professionals and isn't aimed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Your choices and rights

You can block cookies and fonts in your browser, and the page will still be readable. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, or to have it deleted. If you joined the waitlist, we hold your email and optional role. Otherwise we hold nothing tied to you beyond short-lived server logs. You're still welcome to ask at the address above, and we'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If the site starts collecting anything new, such as a sign-up form or analytics, this policy will be updated before that change goes live, and the effective date above will change.
